# DocuGulf Document Attestation Guide

A step-by-step reference guide for document authentication, embassy attestation, and UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) legalization.

---

## What is Document Attestation?
Document attestation is the official verification process required to confirm the authenticity of documents issued outside the UAE. Legalization is mandatory for obtaining employment visas, family residence visas, business licenses, and educational admissions inside the United Arab Emirates.

---

## 4-Step Attestation Process

### Step 1: Notary & Local Government Authentication
* Verification by a registered Notary Public in the document's country of origin.
* State-level authentication (e.g., State Home Department, Human Resource Development/HRD, or Sub-Divisional Magistrate/SDM depending on the country).

### Step 2: Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Origin Country)
* Legalization by the central government authority (e.g., MEA in India, FCDO in the UK, or the Department of State in the US).

### Step 3: UAE Embassy Legalization
* Official verification and stamping by the Embassy or Consulate of the United Arab Emirates located in the country of origin.

### Step 4: UAE MOFA Attestation
* Final electronic or physical attestation inside the United Arab Emirates through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).

| **Commercial** | Articles of Association, board resolutions, power of attorney, trade licenses | Company formation, branch registration, corporate bank account setup |

---

## Important Checklist Before Submitting
- Ensure original physical certificates are intact with no lamination preventing stamps (or arrange for pre-delamination where permitted).
- Prepare clear passport copies of the document holder.
- Ensure personal details (spelling of full name, date of birth) match across all government IDs and certificates.
- Check whether your country requires English/Arabic legal translation before submission.
